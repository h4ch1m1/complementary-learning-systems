`experimental_delayed.csv` contains approximate readings of the filled experimental
points and published confidence-interval endpoints in Howard & Kahana (2002),
Figures 7B and 8A (PDF pages 19 and 21). These data originate in Howard & Kahana
(1999). They are figure digitizations, not raw participant observations.

`y` is the plotted experimental mean. `lower` and `upper` are the paper's 95%
within-subject confidence limits, calculated by its authors using Loftus & Masson
(1994). They are not recomputed intervals or model uncertainty. PFR uses study
positions 1–12; CRP uses first-to-second recall transitions and excludes lag zero.
Source: [Howard & Kahana (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmps.2001.1388),
Figures 7B and 8A. The readings preserve the printed geometry; digitization
precision should not be interpreted as participant-level measurement precision.

`paper_model_points.csv` contains approximate readings of the paper's **model**
curves. It must not be labeled experimental or substituted for this data overlay.
