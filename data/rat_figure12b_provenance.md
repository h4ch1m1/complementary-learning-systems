`rat_figure12b.csv` contains manually estimated square-marker centers and error-bar endpoints from the published normalized panel, rounded to 0.01. They are NOT original numerical observations, individual-animal measurements, or author-supplied values. Approximate reading uncertainty is of order 0.01; no formal extraction-error distribution is assumed. Error-bar endpoints are reproduced as drawn; they are not relabeled as confidence intervals or used as statistical weights.

The original simulation's continuous line is not treated as additional experimental observations.
